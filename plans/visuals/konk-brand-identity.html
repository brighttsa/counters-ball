KONK! — brand identity concept

Wordmark, key art, home-screen title and app icon for a Ghanaian bottle-cap football game. Custom hand-shaped letters; the O is a crown cap caught at the moment it strikes the folded-paper ball. Palette: near-black ink, warm chalk-white, golden yellow, and electric cyan used only for the ball's path.
