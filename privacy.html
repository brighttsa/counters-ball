KONK! Privacy Policy

Updated October 2, 2026

KONK! is a bottle-cap football game by Bright Tsa. The game is designed to work offline and does not sell personal information, track players across apps or websites, or use advertising SDKs.

Data stored on your device

KONK! stores game progress, settings, local match records, player names entered for local play, and Daily Flick results on your device. This information is used only to provide game features and can be removed by deleting the app or clearing its local data.

Online features

Optional sharing, challenge links, Message Match, and Live Match features require an internet connection. Information you deliberately place in a shared challenge or multiplayer session is transmitted only to provide that feature. Do not enter sensitive personal information as a player name or room label.

Optional KONKER profiles store your chosen display name, a random profile identifier, creation/update times and a recovery-secret hash on our Cloudflare-hosted multiplayer service. Your browser stores the profile and private recovery credentials. Downloaded recovery files are private: anyone with the code can access that profile. Clearing browser data does not remove the server profile; contact us below for a profile privacy or removal request.

Room-seat credentials are retained on your device for up to 30 days, for at most ten rooms, to reconnect you to the same seat. Online rooms and their accepted turn records expire after 30 days without activity. Public opponent search uses short-lived tickets and a hashed network identifier to limit abusive requests. It does not provide a verified real-world identity or ranked rating.

Optional live voice

Players in private friend rooms and four-player knockout rooms may choose to join live voice. Voice is off until you select Join voice; your microphone remains off until you select Unmute mic and grant microphone access. Mute stops microphone capture, and leaving the room ends the connection. Public matchmaking voice is not available.

Live voice is delivered through LiveKit Cloud. Audio is transmitted in real time to the other people in that private room. KONK! does not record or transcribe voice conversations. LiveKit processes connection data to provide the service under its own terms and privacy policy. Do not share sensitive information in voice chat.

Waitlist and game feedback

The optional mobile waitlist stores your email, selected platforms, optional name and submission time on our Cloudflare-hosted service. We use it only for KONK! mobile-launch updates. Waitlist entries expire after 180 days. You can request removal at the contact address below.

Game feedback stores your message, experience rating, category, platform, optional device details and submission time for up to 90 days. An email is optional and is used for a feedback reply only with your permission. Feedback does not subscribe you to the waitlist. Please do not include sensitive information. Submission content is private, not posted publicly. Temporary keyed network identifiers help limit spam; raw network addresses are not stored in the submission database.

Diagnostics and third parties

The app does not include third-party advertising, cross-app tracking, or analytics SDKs. Apple may provide developers with aggregated App Store and crash diagnostics according to your device and App Store privacy settings.

Children

KONK! does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The optional online sharing features should be used with a parent or guardian where appropriate.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to brighttsa@gmail.com.
