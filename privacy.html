KONK! Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

KONK! is a bottle-cap football game by Bright Tsa. The game is designed to work offline and does not sell personal information, track players across apps or websites, or use advertising SDKs.

Data stored on your device

KONK! stores game progress, settings, local match records, player names entered for local play, and Daily Flick results on your device. This information is used only to provide game features and can be removed by deleting the app or clearing its local data.

Online features

Optional sharing, challenge links, Message Match, and Live Match features require an internet connection. Information you deliberately place in a shared challenge or multiplayer session is transmitted only to provide that feature. Do not enter sensitive personal information as a player name or room label.

Diagnostics and third parties

The app does not include third-party advertising, cross-app tracking, or analytics SDKs. Apple may provide developers with aggregated App Store and crash diagnostics according to your device and App Store privacy settings.

Children

KONK! does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The optional online sharing features should be used with a parent or guardian where appropriate.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to brighttsa@gmail.com.
