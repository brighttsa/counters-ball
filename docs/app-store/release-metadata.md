## Description

KONK! turns Ghanaian bottle-cap football into a tactile tabletop game built around touch, angles and tiny physical moments.

Flick your caps into position, strike the paper ball, bank shots off improvised obstacles and listen as every surface, collision and goal responds. Play through six distinct venues, each with its own table, atmosphere and Street Legends challenge.

Features:

- Six handcrafted Ghanaian tabletop venues
- Street Legends matches with unique physical rules
- Solo play against adaptive rivals
- Two-player play on one device
- Daily Flick challenges
- Broadcast, Tactical, Street and Free camera views
- Slow-motion goal replays and shareable results
- Detailed tactile sound and original music
- Offline play for local modes

Online access is required only for optional sharing and online match features.

## Review notes

KONK! packages its complete game runtime inside the application and launches at `konk-local://game/`; core gameplay does not require internet access. Optional Live Match, Message Match and sharing features use internet connectivity. No account or login is required. The first screen asks the reviewer to tap once so Web Audio can start. All game modes can then be reached from Home.

The app stores only local progress and settings. It contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, tracking, purchases or subscriptions.
