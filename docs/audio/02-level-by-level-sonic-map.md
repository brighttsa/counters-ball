# 03. Level-by-Level Sonic Map

Only the six implemented campaign venues are included. Street Legends reuses
these locations with one venue-specific table mechanism across three acts.

## Schoolyard Break

| Field | Audit |
|---|---|
| Level | Schoolyard Break; Adabraka Primary, Accra |
| Physical/visual character | Midday playground beside a school wall with louvre windows, blue door, exercise book, school bag, slippers, caps, sachets and hopscotch. |
| Playing surface | Clean corrugated/card stock tabletop. |
| Environmental materials | Packed red earth, plaster, glass louvres, painted door, paper and plastic. Street Legends adds a turning plastic/wooden ruler. |
| Visible sound sources | School door/windows, exercise book, bag, loose caps, ruler, feet implied by break-time setting. |
| Expected human activity | Small nearby group of pupils at break, not a stadium; activity should recede during aiming. |
| Current audio | Soft bright noise bed; procedural distant bounce every 6-18 s and bird every 8-25 s. Normal cardboard/table physics; generic wood knock for ruler turns/impacts. |
| Missing audio | Actual schoolyard depth, sparse pupil movement/reactions, distant bell, door/louvre detail, ruler swivel/edge strike, cardboard motion texture. |
| Proposed sonic identity | **Bed:** airy schoolyard with restrained distant pupils and open exterior tone. **Punctuation:** shoes on packed earth, book/page handling, remote ball bounce. **Signature:** one distant school bell and a short dry ruler pivot/tap. Keep birds rare. Open, dry acoustics. |

| Field | Audit |
|---|---|

## Shared surface map

The player-facing table surfaces are only:

1. **Clean cardboard:** Schoolyard and Kiosk.
2. **Dusty cardboard:** Harmattan; separate movement set because friction and grit
   are mechanically and visually important.
3. **Worn wood:** Veranda, Roadside and Lights Out; one core movement library can
   be filtered subtly by venue acoustics instead of triplicating Foley.

Concrete, earth, asphalt and screed belong to ambience and environmental events;
caps do not move on those ground surfaces during normal gameplay.

## Street Legends mechanism map

| Venue | Implemented mechanism | Required sonic distinction |
|---|---|---|
| Schoolyard | Turning ruler seesaw | Plastic/wood ruler pivot, edge strike, sweep stop. |
| Kiosk | Rotating enamel change dish | Enamel scrape/tick, rim rattle, dish strike. |
| Veranda | Clay-pot maze and bounce-only goals | Hollow clay tap/clack, tiny ceramic wobble, denied-goal dead response. |
| Roadside | Toll booths, booms and signals | Wooden/painted-metal boom latch, booth hit, jam strain. |
| Harmattan | Goals mounted on departing toy lorries | Small wheel chatter, stop click, tailboard/goal frame knock. |
| Lights Out | Ordered coin stacks and padlocked goals | Coin-stack strike progression, compact padlock release, bulb-on accent. |
